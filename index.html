A little clarity

Your questions, answered.

Can a small gift help?

Small gifts contribute to the larger effort to fund clean water projects. $5 is our suggested starting point, and you can choose what works for your budget.

Where does my donation go?

100% of public donations fund clean water projects. Donations are processed on charity: water's official website.

How can I see the impact?

charity: water documents funded projects with photos and GPS coordinates. Visit the official website to learn more.
